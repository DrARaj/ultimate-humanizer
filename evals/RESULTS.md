## Limits

- This measures the linter only. It does not test whether the skill rewrites text well.
- The AI text comes from one model (Claude Haiku) and one plain prompt, one passage per topic. Other models, prompts and edits would give different rates.
- Wikipedia text is written and edited by many people, and some of it follows house style. Abstracts are scientific prose. Neither set stands for all human writing.
- Sample sizes are small (40, 20 and 40 passages), so a rate of a few percent is one or two passages.
- The numbers are not an AI detector. Lint flags are evidence, not proof, and a flagged human passage shows that these tells also occur in ordinary writing.
